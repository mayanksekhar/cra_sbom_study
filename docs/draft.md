## 3 Method

Tools and versions. Syft 1.45.1, Trivy 0.69.3, Grype 0.114.0, cdxgen 12.9.0 (run at CycloneDX 1.6, because Grype and Trivy reject its default 1.7), OSV Scanner 2.7.0, govulncheck 1.9.0 against a local copy of the Go vulnerability database (4,712 entries, last modified 2026-10-27). Go 1.26.8, Maven 3.9.11 on JDK 25, Python 3.14.7, Node 24.18.0. {tools/pinned_versions.txt, tools/dbsnap_*.txt, tools/govuln_20261030.sha256}

Vulnerability data is frozen. Snapshot 20261030 (Grype database built 2026-10-30 06:23 UTC, Trivy database updated 07:16 UTC, OSV databases downloaded the same day) is used for every table. Snapshot 20261026 was used for the pilot's first scans and for the drift measurement. Checksums are recorded.

Projects. Pilot at default branch HEAD, 10 projects in five ecosystems. Older pins, 6 projects at tags from 2024. Go set, 10 applications at tags from February to October 2024 (lazygit excluded, it vendors its dependencies). Maven set, 8 projects (WebGoat dropped, no root pom at the pinned commit). PyPI set, 8 projects at tags from May to October 2024. Rust set, 8 applications at tags from October 2023 to October 2024, all with a committed Cargo.lock. npm set, 11 projects with a package-lock.json (the first three: mocha, TypeScript, pm2; TypeScript has no runtime dependencies, so its shipped set is empty and it contributes only at the all level). The other eight were chosen by a rule fixed before any tool ran (docs/npm_selection.md): the first 8 of 15 listed candidates whose highest stable tag has a committed lockfile of version 2 or 3, namely uptime-kuma 2.5.11, node-red 5.0.9, nest v12.5.0, puppeteer v19.2.2, socket.io 4.7.5, json-server v0.17.4, http-server v14.1.1 and nodemon v3.1.14. The tags of puppeteer, socket.io and json-server are old because their newer releases use tag names the rule does not match. For puppeteer and socket.io the generators list many times more components than the root lock shipped truth (puppeteer 148 and socket.io 20 components); I have not traced why, so the extra findings there measure the truth definition. Pins are in dataset/*_pinned.csv.

Truth. Per ecosystem, from the package manager of the project: Go declared, module graph and runtime on three platforms; Maven all, shipped (runtime scope) and shipped without optional dependencies; npm and Rust all, shipped and whole tree; PyPI lock where a lock exists, else a fresh install (install truth reflects resolution on 2026-11-07, so lockless projects are scored by name only).

Reference and error attribution. A reference SBOM is built from the shipped truth. A generator's findings are compared with the same scanner's findings on the reference, so database differences cancel. Every missed finding is attributed to: component absent, component without a version, wrong version, or scanner miss. Unit: one finding per component and issue (alias cluster). {harness/score_findings.py, harness/count_units.py}

Reachability oracle: govulncheck, Go only. VEX forms: A (product is the application, subcomponent is the vulnerable module) and B (product is the module).

Threats noted here: one version per tool; popular projects with shared dependencies; truth for Maven has up to 3 of 237 components in doubt; for PyPI install truth drifts from the pinned lock (28 of 37 poetry packages, 29 of 33 ansible-lint packages resolve to other versions).

4.4 Maven profiles [P, two minimal controls and two real projects]. 4.5 Identity [V for Go, P elsewhere]. No version: Grype 44, 19, 23, 11, Trivy and OSV Scanner 0. Version field UNKNOWN (what Syft writes): Grype and Trivy 0, OSV Scanner 20, 14, 14, 3. A second matrix (nats-server and caddy, purl without a version in every variant, only the version field varied) shows which field a scanner reads: Trivy reports 0 in every variant including the real tag in the version field (it takes the version from the purl, observed, mechanism not examined), Grype follows the version field (14 and 15 at the tag, 20 and 14 for v0.0.0, 0 for UNKNOWN, latest and v999.0.0, 44 and 19 with no version), OSV Scanner treats UNKNOWN and latest like v0.0.0 (37 and 26 advisory ids, against 28 and 28 at the tag; units differ from the first control) [V for these 2 projects, one version per tool]. An isolation control varied the purl version and the version field independently (nats-server and caddy): Trivy follows the purl only (version in the purl only 14 and 14, in the field only 0 and 0, v0.0.0 in the purl 20 and 14); Grype and OSV Scanner use the version field first and fall back to the purl (tag in the purl only: Grype 14 and 15, OSV Scanner 14 and 15 groups; UNKNOWN in the field with the tag in the purl: Grype 0 and 0, OSV Scanner 20 and 14 groups). With the tag in the purl and no version field all three scanners give 14 on nats-server, and 15, 14 and 15 on caddy [V, 2 Go projects, one version per tool]. Causes not examined. A private docs package named axios at version 1.0.0, nested under the root in cdxgen's SBOM, gave 31 findings in Grype and none in Trivy; with the same component at top level both report 31. ## 5 Scanner consumption

5.1 Every missed finding traces to the SBOM [V]. 1266 reference findings over seven sets, scanner misses 0 in all 21 generator rows (Grype as scanner; OSV Scanner is not scored and treats a placeholder version differently, see 4.5). Findings recovered: pilot, cdxgen 22 of 22, Syft 5 of 22 (16 component absent, 1 no version), Trivy 22 of 22; older pins 48 of 48 for all three; Go, cdxgen 606 of 608 (2 absent, both in fzf), Syft and Trivy 608; Maven, cdxgen and Trivy 201 of 201, Syft 43 of 201 (120 absent, 38 no version); PyPI, cdxgen 61 of 80 (19 wrong version: it resolved newer versions than the pinned lock), Syft 73 of 80 (7 absent), Trivy 54 of 80 (26 absent); Rust, cdxgen 47 of 47, Syft 47 of 47, Trivy 46 of 47 (1 absent component, bottom); npm, cdxgen 260 of 260, Syft 260 of 260, Trivy 257 of 260 (3 absent components, all in nest). The Rust reference is concentrated in starship (28 of 47) and npm holds 260 findings over 11 projects (uptime-kuma 79, puppeteer 51, json-server 32, pm2 32, mocha 23, the other six 43), so the Rust set says little about differences between generators on findings, and the npm set shows one: Trivy's SBOM for nest. {docs/table_findings.md}

5.2 Scanner difference on identical input [V, one snapshot]. Trivy reports none of 17 findings that Grype reports on 5 of the 10 Go projects (caddy 3, cli 3, helm 5, hugo 1, k9s 5). 14 of them are reachable findings (6 percent of 230 reachable, module level classes, ten projects; not recomputed at product level). Trivy's database was built after Grype's, so staleness does not explain it. OSV confirmed the first 12; the k9s 5 were not checked. Mechanism unknown. For dependency findings only; for the product itself the three scanners agree (5.3).

5.3 Findings on the product itself [V]. 53 findings on the 10 Go applications' own module at the pinned tag; 52 are confirmed by Grype, Trivy and OSV, 52 have a record in the Go database for the module path, 18 are high or critical in Grype. Generators exclude the root from the component list, so these are outside the inventory. Three of the four projects with none have no database record at all. {results_go/product_findings.csv}

5.4 Database drift [P]. Reference findings in the pilot grew from 19 to 22 between snapshots four days apart.

5.5 Compatibility [V]. cdxgen 12.9.0 writes CycloneDX 1.7 by default, which Grype and Trivy reject. Syft SBOMs for five npm and PyPI projects crash OSV Scanner 2.7.0 (unsupported ecosystem GitHub Actions, then a nil pointer); copies without the GitHub Actions components scan.

5.6 Container images, operating system packages only [V; one build of each image, one snapshot]. Eight images, pulled by tag with the digests recorded (Debian 12 and Debian 11 slim of 26 September 2024, Alpine 3.20.3 and 3.18.9, nginx 1.27.2, redis 7.4.1, python 3.12.7 slim, postgres 16.4 on Alpine 3.20), carry 88, 96, 14, 15, 149, 89, 105 and 45 installed packages. Truth is the dpkg or apk database read from the exported image. The reference SBOM is built from it, with the source package in the purl and in Trivy's own source properties, both derived from the database. Syft 1.45.1 and Trivy 0.69.3 list every one of these packages at name level on all eight images (0 extra, 0 missed). cdxgen 12.9.0 lists every one as well (0 missed) and adds, on every image, extra OS components (37, 43, 3, 4, 81, 38, 44 and 15), every one of them the name of a source package of the truth. Its image data comes from a Trivy binary: its code calls one, its log carries Trivy's own log lines and its components carry Trivy properties. No bundled binary was installed and the Trivy on the path is the 0.69.3 scored here, so its package list is probably not independent of Trivy's (the code that picks the binary when none is bundled was not read). The reference holds 2333 findings under Grype 0.114.0 and 2504 under Trivy 0.69.3. Calibration: each scanner on the image equals the same scanner on the reference on all eight images, except 21 findings that Grype reports only through a CPE on busybox (3, 14 and 4 on Alpine 3.20.3, Alpine 3.18.9 and postgres) and 8 findings on the nginx.org build of nginx that Trivy reports from an SBOM and not from the image. Scoring: Grype finds 2333 of 2333 (100.0 percent) from Syft's SBOMs, 336 (14.4 percent) from Trivy's and 358 (15.3 percent) from cdxgen's; Trivy finds 2504 of 2504 (100.0 percent) from its own SBOMs, 398 (15.9 percent) from Syft's and 0 (0.0 percent) from cdxgen's. The 966 extra findings Grype reports on cdxgen's SBOMs are probably findings on those source package components (checked once, on debian12, outside the repository's scripts). Controls: adding the upstream qualifier to the purls of Trivy's SBOMs lets Grype find 2200 of 2333 (all five Debian images), adding the Alpine distro value lets it find 2333 of 2333, adding the upstream qualifier to cdxgen's purls lets Grype find 2333 of 2333, and copying Trivy's source properties onto Syft's SBOMs lets Trivy find 2504 of 2504, with no extra finding in the controls on Trivy's and Syft's SBOMs. For Trivy reading cdxgen's SBOMs, an operating system component, and that component with a dependency graph, both leave the count at 0 and the cause is not established. Each of Syft's and Trivy's SBOMs is complete for the scanner of its own project and carries the source package in a form the other scanner does not read, and cdxgen's, like Trivy's, lacks the upstream qualifier Grype needs. This holds for default output at these versions and is not a ranking of generators. Software installed outside the package manager is not covered and each configuration was run once. {results_containers/*.csv, harness/container_*.py}

## 6 VEX and reachability

6.1 Reachability on Go [P, single oracle, no agreement measured]. Product level (govulncheck on the packages of the shipped binary, ten applications): 608 findings, 215 called, 368 not called, 25 without a verdict, so 368 of 608 (61 percent) are not called. k9s needed a raised memory limit with swap (peak about 8.2 GB resident, 14 minutes), and its product level classes equal its module level classes for all 112 findings. Module level (first run, govulncheck over every package of each module, ten applications): 608 findings on the reference SBOMs of 10 applications: 230 called, 356 not called, 22 without a verdict (16 have no Go database record). Reachability would remove 356 of 608. The module level run overstated what is called: on the ten projects 230 called findings became 215 (12 moved to not called, 3 to unknown). A name search for the vulnerable symbols in 20 sampled not called findings found no plausible missed call in the 18 testable cases (weak test: no reflection or interface dispatch). A mechanical check against the product level classes (symbols of the vulnerable functions in the built product binaries, linux amd64, default flags, one build) covers the findings whose Go database record has import entries: Of the 305 not called findings that can be checked this way, 256 have no vulnerable function in the built binary; of the 109 called findings that can be checked, 1 has none (cli, go-chi/chi GO-2026-4316: govulncheck reports three function level traces to the gh binary, through middleware.Write and two package init functions, none to the listed symbol RedirectSlashes, which is not linked; the two methods compare different code for this advisory, reason not examined). A first version of this check had a matching fault for vendored dependencies (symbols carry a vendor/ prefix, restic vendors) that produced one more contradiction, fixed. On the module level classes the same check gave 250 of 293 and 8 of 121; 6 of those 8 are no longer called when govulncheck runs on the product package, and a seventh was the vendor prefix fault [V, results_go/mech_result_product.csv, results_go/mech_result.csv]. It is a consistency check by a second method, not an independent validation, and it cannot reach the findings without a Go database id. Agreement with manual labels is not measured: the author's first sitting on 10 cards gave 10 unsure (docs/pilot_findings.md). {results_go/reach_product.json, results_go/reach.json, results_go/mech_result.csv, labels/label_sample.csv}

6.4 Cross project suppression [V]. A module scoped statement from project P suppresses reachable findings in project Q when both contain the same module version and advisory. Product level, ten projects: 5 of 90 ordered pairs overlap; 37 of 215 called findings are exposed (cli 9, hugo 15, k9s 13); the static prediction matched the scans of all 5 pairs in both scanners (13, 13, 9, 9 and 2 called findings removed). Module level, ten projects (first run): 5 of 90 ordered pairs overlap; 42 of 230 called findings are exposed; the static prediction matched the scans of all 5 pairs in both scanners (13 or 14 called findings removed in the four large pairs, 2 in the fifth). Product scoped form A removed none in either run. Exposure depends on shared module versions in this set. {results_go/vex_cross_product.txt, harness/vex_cross.py}
